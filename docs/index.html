China Work Permit Points Reference & SAFEA Classification Guide (2026)

Foreign professionals applying for a mainland China Z-visa and Foreigner's Work Permit are evaluated under the State Administration of Foreign Experts Affairs (SAFEA) three-tier talent framework: Category A (High-end Talent, ≥ 85 points), Category B (Professional Talent, 60–84 points), and Category C (Temporary or Seasonal Personnel, < 60 points). For applicants without an accredited bachelor's degree or without two full years of post-graduation work experience, clearing the strict 60-point scoring threshold is the primary legal path to a Category B approval. This technical reference documents the official scoring weights, regional adjustment bands, and verification procedures implemented by ChinaVisaCalc Z Visa Points Calculator.

1. Official Tier Thresholds & Direct Qualification Shortcuts

Applicants do not always need to pass the points table if they satisfy an automatic Category A or Category B exemption criterion under current municipal Foreign Experts Bureau rules. Direct-qualification tracks coexist alongside the 100-point rubric:

  • Category A (≥ 85 points or direct salary/award track): Monthly salary reaching 6× the prior-year municipal average social wage triggers automatic Category A qualification regardless of age or degree points, unlocking priority processing (typically 5 working days) and paperless criminal background declarations in tier-one hubs such as Shanghai and Beijing.
  • Category B (60–84 points or Bachelor + 2 years experience): Most corporate hires qualify via a notarized Bachelor's degree paired with 24 months of verifiable post-degree full-time experience matching the job role. When either credential is missing, candidates can audit alternative score combinations through the 60-point strategy calculator.
  • Category C (< 60 points): Strictly quota-controlled for seasonal border roles, internships under bilateral agreements, and temporary service entries, with single-term caps and non-renewable limits in most provinces. See the Category A vs B vs C compliance comparison for exact quota constraints.

3. Non-Degree Workarounds & Salary-Driven Threshold Calculations

A common engineering question among founders, startup hires, and specialized technical contractors is whether a bachelor's degree is legally mandatory when total points reach 60. While standard degree-based Category B track requires a degree, the SAFEA points-accumulation track permits applicants with zero education points (0/20) to reach 60+ points by combining strong compensation (≥ 250,000 RMB = 11–17 pts), extensive tenure (10+ years = 13 pts), 9+ months residency (15 pts), prime age 26–45 (15 pts), and HSK certification (3–5 pts). 4. Document Sequence: Notification Letter, Z-Visa, and Residence Permit

Even with 65 verified points, missing a single chronological step causes customs or Public Security Bureau (PSB) Exit-Entry rejection. Follow the mandatory four-stage sequence documented in the Z-visa application workflow and work visa types handbook:

  1. Pre-Entry Apostille & Non-Criminal Record: Since China joined the Hague Apostille Convention, Hague member nationals obtain an Apostille on their diploma and national police clearance within 6 months prior to filing instead of double embassy legalization.
  2. Work Permit Notification Letter (外国人来华工作许可通知): Filed online by the sponsoring Chinese entity; approval generates a digital QR notification valid for entry-visa issuance.
  3. Single-Entry Z-Visa Conversion: Present the Notification Letter at a Chinese embassy or visa center abroad. The Z-visa carries a strict 30-day window after mainland entry to register temporary residence and undergo medical examination.
  4. Physical Work Permit Card & PSB Residence Permit: Within 15 days of arrival, complete the physical Work Permit application, then submit passport biometrics at the municipal PSB within 30 days to receive a 1-to-5-year Work Residence Permit allowing unlimited international reentry.
